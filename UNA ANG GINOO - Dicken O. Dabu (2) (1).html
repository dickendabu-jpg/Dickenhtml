Walking in the Light Online

Notes on faith, integrity, and what I choose to post.

Why I Put God First on the Internet

Every post, comment, and share is a small choice. I want my choices online to match what I believe: honesty, kindness, and respect for others. When I log in, I ask one question: does this bring light or does it add noise?

"Let your light shine before others, that they may see your good deeds." – Matthew 5:16

My Three Promises

Integrity

I will not copy, cheat, or pretend to be someone I am not.

Kindness

I will pause before I reply, and speak words that build up.

Responsibility

I will check facts before I share and protect other people's privacy.

About Me

Hello! I am Dicken O. Dabu, a first-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) student from Section 31. I am passionate about learning technology, developing new skills, and discovering how creativity and programming can make a positive difference in people's lives.

I am currently learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to improve my web development skills and turn my ideas into meaningful and creative projects. I believe that these skills will help me build useful websites, develop my own projects, and create more opportunities for my future career in technology.

Aside from programming, I am also a musician. I have experience playing different musical instruments, including the drums, bass guitar, keyboard, and electric guitar. I also want to use my technology skills to serve our church, especially in the media ministry, by helping create and improve digital presentations, websites, and other media-related projects.

My goal is to continue learning, improving my abilities, and using what I learn to serve others. I believe that technology and creativity are valuable tools that can make a positive impact in the community.

My guiding Bible verse is Matthew 5:14 – “You are the light of the world.” This verse reminds me to use my talents, knowledge, and abilities to become a positive influence and share God's light with others.

Be Kind. Be True. Be Careful.

A short guide to honest and respectful behavior online.

Three Everyday Digital Habits

Online kindness

Before you comment, ask: Is it true? Is it kind? Is it needed? Disagree without insulting.

Honesty in creation

Credit your sources. Do not copy another person's work or images without permission.

Protecting others

Do not share private photos, messages, or rumors. Report bullying to a trusted adult.

Do and Don't

DoDon't
Verify before sharingSpread unchecked news
Encourage othersMock or shame
Cite your sourcesPlagiarize
Use strong passwordsShare your login

Quick Check

You see a classmate's embarrassing photo being shared. What should you do?

Show the answer

Do not share it. Tell the classmate and a teacher or trusted adult, and help stop the spread.
